About Med4All

Built in Ghana, for Ghana’s health system.

Med4All was created by PharmAccess and the Christian Health Association of Ghana to make quality-assured medicines more accessible and affordable for every health facility.

Explore the marketplace Contact us
Vision and mission

A future where every healthcare provider and patient can reliably access quality-assured, affordable medicines when and where they are needed.

Our mission

To connect healthcare providers with trusted suppliers through a digital procurement platform that simplifies ordering, strengthens supply visibility, improves affordability and availability, and increasingly uses data and technology to enable better decisions.

Our story

Fixing a broken supply chain, one order at a time.

Health facilities in Ghana have long faced high prices, unreliable supply and doubts about medicine quality. Med4All was set up to shorten the chain, connecting trusted suppliers directly to health facilities with full transparency.

Today it serves health facilities across the CHAG network and is opening to private hospitals, clinics and pharmacies nationwide.

  1. 2017The partnership begins

    CHAG and PharmAccess start working with Ghana’s FDA on a digital supply chain for quality essential medicines.

  2. 2019Med4All launches

    The platform launches in Accra, connecting health facilities to suppliers at negotiated prices.

  3. 2021Financing for health facilities

    An initiative helps CHAG facilities on Med4All pay for the medicines they order.

  4. 2023Tech4Good finalist

    Finalist for the Inclusive Health Award at the Tech4Good Awards.

  5. 2025New platform

    Med4All moves to a new ordering platform, with approvals, payment options and delivery confirmation built into every order.

  6. 2026AEIP winner

    One of three winning teams in the Africa-Europe Innovation Platform Public Health challenge.

  7. NextSmarter procurement

    Building toward a procurement intelligence platform, with better supply visibility and data-led decisions.
