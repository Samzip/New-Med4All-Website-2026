This Privacy Policy applies to anyone using the Med4All platform (the “Platform”) (https://med4all.org.gh/), a digitally supported procurement system for medicines. It is developed by PharmAccess Foundation (“PharmAccess”) together with the Christian Health Association of Ghana (“CHAG”). PharmAccess is a not-for-profit organization dedicated to connecting more people to better health care in Africa established under the laws of the Netherlands with its registered office address at Paasheuvelweg 25, 1105 BP Amsterdam. CHAG is a network organization of 350 healthcare facilities and health training institutions owned by 34 different Christian church denominations. CHAG is also a recognized implementation partner of the Ministry of Health (MOH), and works within the policies, guidelines, and strategies of the MOH. Nonetheless it is autonomous and takes an independent opinion on healthcare improvements. It has its registered office at 21 Jubilee Well Street, Labone, Accra.

Med4All is strongly dedicated to protecting the privacy of its users and processes personal data in accordance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (the “GDPR”) and the Data Protection Act Ghana, 2012 (the “DPAG”), where the provisions offering users a greater amount of protection shall in any case prevail. This Privacy Policy will explain how Med4All uses the personal data collected when you use the Med4All Platform.

Personal data collected

Collected data could include:

  • personal identification information (Name, email address, username, phone number);
  • a personal bank account number, only in those cases where the bank account is used for facility purposes and the facility does not have a business bank account;
  • platform usage data and other related technical information (for example: number of logins, average session length, usage per week);
  • any other information you provide to us.

Data collection

Med4All collects data about you when you:

  • are registered to our Platform;
  • use or visit our Platform;
  • communicate with us (via email, phone, postal mail, etc.);
  • provide us with feedback;
  • otherwise provide your details to us either online or offline (for example by sending us an email).

Legal basis

We are only allowed to process your personal data if there is a lawful ground for processing. Therefore, we only process your personal data if one of the following grounds apply:

  • if the processing of data is necessary for the adequate performance of the contract between you and Med4All. Without such information, we may not be able to fully provide you with our services;
  • if the processing of data is necessary to comply with a legal obligation to which Med4All is subject.

Purpose of data processing

Med4All collects personal data from you only in a fair, reasonable and lawful manner. The collected personal data will only be used for the following purposes:

  • to process your order and manage your account;
  • to facilitate an efficient, secure, and on-time payment process;
  • to contact you, update you or provide you with information;
  • to improve our Platform and understand its use for statistical purposes (number of site visits, average time visitors spend at the site, user journey when using the Platform) and security purposes.

Data security

Med4All takes the security of your personal data very seriously and has taken adequate technical and organizational measures to protect the collected data from accidental and unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to personal data.

The measures taken include, but are not limited to:

  • restricting access to personal data through technically enforced user permissions;
  • user authentication requiring usernames and passwords to meet complexity standards;
  • storing data in the cloud, protected by fully up-to-date security measures.

Data transfer and third party usage

Med4All will transfer data to a secured cloud server in the Netherlands, a country with an adequate level of security under GDPR. Med4All will put in place adequate measures to ensure the security of such personal data. Med4All shall provide you with the specific information about your data transfer out of Ghana upon request.

Med4All does not sell, trade or rent personal data to third parties. We only share data with trusted third-party service providers and only if that data is strictly necessary to carry out their services. We may use the third-party service providers named below to process and store your data:

  • Microsoft Azure, which hosts our Platform and which securely stores collected data where necessary. Read their privacy policy [link];
  • Bluehost (part of Endurance corporation), which we use to manage our communications. Read their privacy policy [link];
  • Google Analytics, a cookie service. Read their privacy policy [link];
  • Fidelity Bank, which executes payments to and from user accounts. Read their privacy policy [link];

Med4All has signed Data Processing Agreements with its third-party service providers to govern such processing and to ensure that the personal data concerned is protected and all GDPR and DPAG requirements are met.

Some of the gathered information, including the usage per week and average session length may also be shared with the CHAG and its denominations. Furthermore, aggregated data might be shared with donors. Information in aggregated form will be visible to all participating users on the reporting page of the Med4All Platform, making it possible for them to compare their overall performance and engagement (e.g. average number of days between stock takes and average number of days between receiving and accepting/rejecting a pro-forma invoice) to those of the other participating users. All data collected may also, in anonymized form, be used by PharmAccess for purpose of publications contributing to PharmAccess’ goals and mission.

Med4All will not share your personal data with any other third-party other than those mentioned in this Privacy Policy or unless we have your permission or the law requires Med4All to do so.

Websites of third parties

The Med4All Platform may contain links to other websites, such as news sites, international organizations, other non-governmental organizations or other relevant entities. This Privacy Policy does not apply to any hyperlinks or content of third-party websites shown on or linked to our Platform. Med4All does not monitor or control the information collection or privacy practices of these or any other third parties and is therefore not responsible for the content, the accuracy, completeness, usefulness of information or the products or services offered by third party websites.

Cookies

Cookies are small (text) files that are stored on your computer when you visit a website. Med4All uses functional and analytical cookies. Med4All does not use any tracking cookies. If we collect personal data about you using cookies, that data is processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy. Functional cookies are necessary for the navigation through our Platform and do not require consent. Med4All uses Google Analytics as an analytical cookie service to record how our Platform is used and to improve our services. Please note that Med4All has adjusted the Google Analytics settings as recommended by the Dutch Data Protection Authority, where:

  • Part of a user’s IP-address captured on Google Analytics is hidden from Med4All;
  • Sharing data through Google Analytics with Google is switched off;
  • Visibility of user ID’s is switched off;
  • Med4All is not using other Google services in combination with Google Analytics-cookies;
  • There is a Data Processing Agreement with Google.

With these settings in place, no consent is required. However, by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on you can still opt-out of the use of Google Analytics cookies.

Data retention

Med4All does not retain your personal data any longer than necessary or as prescribed by law. You may unsubscribe from Med4All’s communications at any given time by following instructions we provide you with in any email we send, or by sending your request by email to customersuccessteam@med4all.org.gh. After ending our newsletter subscription, your personal data will be deleted from our communication list.

Your rights

Med4All gladly informs you of your rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to be informed;
  • Right of access;
  • Right to rectification;
  • Right to erasure;
  • Right to limitation of data processing;
  • Right to data portability;
  • Right to object to the collection of personal data;
  • Right to lodge a complaint (with the Dutch independent supervisory authority, ‘Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens’, or the Data Protection Commission Ghana).

In case you would like to invoke any of your rights above, please contact: privacyofficer@pharmaccess.org. We shall respond to such requests within 21 days.

Modifications

This Privacy Policy has been last modified on November 12th, 2020. Med4All reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any future changes made to this Privacy Policy will be posted on our Platform.

Contact information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact Med4All at:
